## 四项P0关闭实现

### 1. 冻结且分离authority root

owner、authority、migration/backup/restore、D21、stop/rollback、retirement、environment、legal scope与holdout registry写入独立`frozen-authority-root.yaml`。runner通过`--authority-root`单独接收，重算canonical root digest，并要求命中代码内固定摘要`sha256:45e499c190c0ce7dc0986120b6a9a0027c87fb5059cb7f5266fbcab2d983904a`。scenario只作用于base state；任何冻结registry差异产生`FROZEN_AUTHORITY_ROOT_MISMATCH`。把agent-self同步写成human并重封migration、restore或D21证据不再能够闭环自证。

### 2. rollout scope、runtime与effect逐项绑定

`allowed_tenants/tasks/actions`必须非空，其canonical `scope_digest`同时绑定approval authority。每条trial、task、schedule、webhook与effect都包含tenant、task type、action和owner，并逐项解引用rollout允许集与active operations owner。任何`authorized=false` effect均产生`UNAUTHORIZED_EFFECT`，不再只拦外部effect；错误scope另产生相应binding失败。

### 3. lifecycle stage与运行终态闭合

offline fixture无条件拒绝`PRODUCTION`。`PAUSED/RETIRED/ROLLED_BACK`要求admission为PAUSED/CLOSED、queue CLEAR、workers STOPPED，且无QUEUED/ACTIVE task与ACTIVE schedule/webhook。ROLLED_BACK时runtime active release、rollback target与verified receipt三者必须一致；候选仍活跃时硬失败。

### 4. 环境、法域与holdout证据可解引用

restore proof只保存`environment_ref`并解引用冻结environment registry；legal review只保存`legal_scope_ref`并校验允许环境、region/scope、active risk owner及时窗。holdout trial与scenario均保存holdout ref，记录绑定task/release、lineage digest、isolation manifest digest、blind grader、gold exposure、contamination与active capability owner。纯合成输入仍保持representative real-world为0。
